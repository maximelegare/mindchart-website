Knowledge graph

The graph is a spatial view of the same data you write in notes.

What appears

Section titled “What appears”
  • Thoughts as nodes, clustered by category or other relations.
  • Notes as containers you can open from a node.
  • People, feelings, and locations when you attach them to a note.

From a thought you can open its graph neighborhood; from a note you can jump into the graph focused on that note.

Why it exists

Section titled “Why it exists”

Lists are good for writing. A graph is better for “what else is like this?” — especially once categories and embeddings are in place.

Layout and similarity use local embeddings. Nothing is uploaded to build the graph.
