Your notes hold connections.

Get startedHow it works

Finding a note is easy. Connecting the dots is not.

Unlike search, which helps you find specific notes, MindChart connects individual thoughts across your notes to reveal hidden patterns, uncover unexpected relationships, and help you see the bigger picture.

What you get

A feature rich app providing an enticing way to take notes

Local-first by design

Access and manage your notes without an internet connection. Your data stays on your device, always available wherever you are.

Fully encrypted

Your notes are fully encrypted and can only be seen inside the app, by you.

Private, separate workspaces

Keep your personal and work life separate, with each workspace securely encrypted and ready to export whenever you need it.

Feature rich text editor

Write freely, structure your ideas, and refine your notes with a full-featured text editor. Your thoughts deserve more than a title and a snippet.

Visual graphs

Visualize individual ideas from your notes in an interactive graph. Discover meaningful connections and hidden patterns that help you see the bigger picture.

PIN-protected app lock

Add an optional PIN lock to keep your notes private. You can choose to use your phone’s lock screen or a custom PIN for additional privacy.

On-device AI

Turn voice recordings or raw text into structured notes with on-device AI. Everything runs locally on your phone, with no cloud services or internet connection required.
